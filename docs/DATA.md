# Database and evidence

The BacRootFit interface catalogs 2,072 unique protein records: 1,036 inherited experimental positives linked to 21 studies and 1,036 reviewed annotation-screened bacterial background proteins. A record can link to more than one study. The labels have different evidentiary meanings: a background annotation does not establish a null root-colonization phenotype, and positive experimental effects depend on the original assay.

The searchable site contains IDs, source identifiers, organism or strain, annotation, study and literature links. It does not serve the database protein sequences or external functional evaluation cohorts. Use the linked source references to investigate experimental context. Metadata availability does not guarantee that every record has the same depth of original evidence or a direct UniProt mapping.

The background was selected from UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot release 2026_03 with sequence quality screening, exclusion of known root-related evidence, functional-name diversity and sequence similarity screening. Functional categories are annotation-based approximations, not experimentally proven unrelated functions.
